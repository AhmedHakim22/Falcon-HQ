## 2. Root Cause of Previous Failures in Notion

### Why do workspaces in Notion turn into "Idea Graveyards"?
1. **The Curse of Infinite Flexibility:** Because Notion can be anything, it ends up being poorly optimized for everything. A user opening Notion must mentally orient themselves before writing.
2. **Missing Guardrails:** When writing a video script in Notion, an inexperienced creator or tired founder omits the Hook or writes a rambling Call to Action because the page does not enforce a structure.
3. **No Dopamine Feedback Loop:** In Notion, finishing a great script feels identical to writing a grocery list. There is no celebration of velocity, no progress bar, and no team milestone badge.
4. **Disconnection from E-commerce Reality:** Notion does not care if a phone case design sold 50 units or 0 units. In Falcon HQ, the business outcome is the ultimate judge of creative quality.
