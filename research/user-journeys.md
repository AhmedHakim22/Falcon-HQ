# Stage 1: User Journeys & Experience Mapping

**Document Path:** `research/user-journeys.md`  
**Date:** 2026-10-21  
**Project:** FALCON HQ  

---

## 1. Persona 1: The Founder / Brand Strategist (Omar)
- **Role:** Brand Architect, Product Designer, Commercial Decision Maker.
- **Context:** Constantly on the move, checking competitors, discovering viral TikTok audios, reviewing manufacturing samples.
- **Pain Point:** Has 20 ideas a day, forgets 18 of them, and struggles to see if his creative team executed the right vision.

### Journey: The On-the-Go Quick Capture & Evaluation
1. **Trigger:** Omar spots a viral reel featuring a 3D textured phone case unboxing.
2. **Step 1 (0–5s):** Taps "Falcon HQ" shortcut on mobile home screen. App opens instantly (PWA cached shell).
3. **Step 2 (5–15s):** Hits "+ فكرة سريعة" (Quick Capture). Pastes reel URL, types title *"ريفيو كفر بارز ضد الصدمات"*, selects category *Product / Reel*.
4. **Step 3 (15–30s):** Rates Priority factors using 4 slider taps (Goal Fit: 5, Customer Value: 4, Reach: 4, Ease: 3) $\rightarrow$ Priority Score: 85 (High).
5. **Step 4 (30–35s):** Selects Route: *"Start Script"* and assigns to Collaborator with note: *"ركز على صوت الخبطة في الأول"*.
6. **Reward:** System displays subtle success toast: `+5 XP تم حفظ الفكرة وتوجيهها للسكريبت`.
7. **Time Elapsed:** 35 seconds total.

---

## 2. Persona 2: The Content Creator / Scriptwriter (Salma)
- **Role:** Copywriter, Script Architect, Hook Researcher.
- **Context:** Works on desktop laptop or tablet, prefers focused writing environments, hates dealing with spreadsheet columns.
- **Pain Point:** Stares at empty Notion docs; forgets copywriting structures; unclear whether Omar wants humor, urgency, or storytelling.

### Journey: Structured Scriptwriting via AIDA + Open Loops
1. **Trigger:** Salma logs into Falcon HQ on desktop and sees 1 new prioritized idea in her "Waiting for Script" queue.
2. **Step 1 (0–1m):** Opens idea details; reads Omar's notes and watches the attached reference video.
3. **Step 2 (1–2m):** Clicks *"افتح استوديو السكريبت"*. Selects framework **AIDA (الانتباه، الاهتمام، الرغبة، الإجراء)** and activates **Open Loops (Light)**.
4. **Step 3 (2–8m):** Guided fields appear in Arabic with thinking prompts:
   - **Attention (الهوك + Open Loop):** *"في سر معظم الناس ما تعرفوش عن الجرابات الشفافة..."*
   - **Interest (الاهتمام):** Explains why standard cases turn yellow within 3 weeks.
   - **Desire (الرغبة):** Showcases Falcon's UV-protected crystal case with custom laser engraving.
   - **Loop Payoff & Action (الإجراء):** Closes the loop, answers the secret, and presents CTA: *"اطلب ديزاينك بخصم 15% من اللينك في البايو"*.
5. **Step 4 (8–10m):** Salma reviews word count, speaking time estimate (~45 seconds), and clicks *"إرسال للمراجعة"*.
6. **Reward:** Status updates to `Review Ready`, Omar receives notification/badge, Salma receives `+25 XP`.
7. **Time Elapsed:** 10 minutes from idea to completed production script.

---

## 3. Collaborative Review & Outcome Attribution Journey
1. **Review:** Omar opens the script on his phone, leaves an inline comment on the Hook (*"خلّي البداية أسرع بكلمتين"*), Salma updates it, and Omar marks it `Approved / Ready to Film`.
2. **Production:** Video is filmed and posted on Instagram Reels.
3. **Results Entry (7 days later):** Omar returns to Falcon HQ $\rightarrow$ Idea Details $\rightarrow$ *Business Results*:
   - Posts Published Link.
   - Attributed Orders: 34 cases.
   - Attributed Revenue: 11,500 EGP.
   - Attribution Confidence: High (Custom promo code used).
   - Core Lesson: *"الـ Hook اللي فيه مقارنة الاصفرار جاب 4 أضعاف التفاعل مقارنة بالفيديوهات العادية."*
4. **Outcome Celebration:** Both members receive `+15 XP` for validated business outcome. The learning is tagged and surfaced as a reusable pattern for future scripts.
