## [0.1.0-MVP] - 2026-10-21
### Added
- **Production Build Verified:** Compiled with Next.js 15.5.28, React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
- **Bento Grid Dashboard:** Warm sand canvas (`#F8F5F0`), white ivory cards (`rounded-3xl`), micro sparkline charts, and live sales metrics ($14,700$ EGP attributed).
- **15-Second Quick Capture Modal & Mobile FAB:** Instant capture with URL parsing, category selection, and multi-track routing (+5 XP).
- **Idea Priority Engine:** Weighted 4-factor scoring with strict `Not Rated` guardrail.
- **Script Studio:** 4 Copywriting Frameworks (AIDA, PASTOR, Story Arc, THEDOC, Freeform), Open Loops Engine (Off/Light/Full), estimated speaking time counter, and async review handoff (+15 XP / +25 XP).
- **Business Outcomes Ledger:** Tracks attributed phone case orders, revenue in EGP, qualitative takeaways, and winning pattern tags.
- **Balanced Gamification:** Transparent XP ledger, level progression, grace-day streaks, and shared team quests.
- **Architectural Documentation:** System design, PostgreSQL schema, RLS permission model, and 4 ADRs (`architecture/`).
- **Quality & Security Reports:** Completed Stage 10 quality verification and Stage 11 security audit with PASS verdict.
